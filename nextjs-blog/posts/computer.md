I have always liked computers, some of my first memories are of them, and they have made sense to me in ways that most things don’t, most things are fluid and move into each other or have a *gradient of answers*. 

But computers don't, the **logic** they follow is the strictest I’ve interacted with, even a car engine does not follow as strict of logic, as with time the materials move around slightly and during long hours of use its ability to function will lessen as it becomes tired. 

Not that computers don’t have their eccentricity, their pure **logic** doesn’t line up with our *reasoning*, just because we have done every thing that *reasonably* is needed for something to work, that doesn’t mean that in the **logic** of the computer it will.
