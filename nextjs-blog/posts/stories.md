I want to tell stories, stories are the fundamental unit that I use to understand the world around me, but I don’t have any place to put them, right now they only live in my head, I’ve made a few attempts to pen them but I always get in my way. I'm hoping by giving them a ‘place,’ even if its only a little website that only I have access to, maybe that will help me build something bit by bit.

The stories I want to express

*Comic*
- **Space Cowboys and the Axe**

A mix of Firefly and Cowboy Bebop, the main thing I want to say with this story is no matter how deep your sin or what atrocities you’ve partaken in, trying to do good and be a better person matters.

*World Building*
- **The People and Places around the City**

Don’t really know how to explain this one but I want to talk about how people live, what they believe, how their society operates, and the rise and fall of the powers that be, in and around this fictional city.
